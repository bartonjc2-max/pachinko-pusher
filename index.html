Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap to drop a coin. The swinging bar aims it through the pachinko pins onto the pusher shelves. Coins pushed off the front edge earn tickets; coins that fall into the side slots are lost.

What is Pin Frenzy?

Every pin hit fills the rainbow meter. When it is full, Pin Frenzy starts: coins off the edge earn double tickets and the gold pocket opens for one Mega Bonus.

I ran out of coins.

A free coin arrives every 30 seconds while you have fewer than 10. You can also collect the daily reward, finish daily goals, or watch an optional ad from the BONUS button.

What are tickets for?

Spend tickets in the Shop on permanent upgrades. Tickets and coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

Can I turn off flashing lights or vibration?

Yes. Open MENU and set FLASHING to LOW and HAPTICS to OFF. Calm lights also turn on automatically when iOS Reduce Motion is enabled.

Email us with your device model, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help.
