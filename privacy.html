Privacy Policy

Pachinko Pusher · Effective November 14, 2026

Summary

Pachinko Pusher does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, tickets, upgrades, daily goals, settings (music, sound, haptics, flashing) and the coins on the table are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Watching an ad is always optional: ads appear only when you choose a BONUS reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version and screen size
  • The device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps, completions) and diagnostic data

Google's use of this data is described in its How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: on iOS 14.5 and later the game asks permission before ads can use your advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice from the game's privacy options.

Children

Pachinko Pusher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

No real-money gambling

Coins and tickets are game items with no cash value. They cannot be bought back, exchanged for money, or redeemed for prizes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@toasterfireapps.com.
